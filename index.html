Questions people ask before their first night

Does Snorly record all night?

No. It listens all night but saves only the seconds around detected snoring, sleep talk or coughing, so a typical night is a handful of clips, not an 8-hour file.

Will it drain the battery?

Keep the phone plugged in. Detection is designed to run all night on a charger; the screen dims to a clock so the display is not the main draw.

Can it tell me if I have sleep apnea?

No app can diagnose sleep apnea. Snorly flags breathing patterns such as pauses and gasps as something worth showing a doctor, and its PDF report gives you a record to bring along. See snoring vs sleep apnea.

Does it work if my partner snores too?

It records whoever is loudest near the phone. Put the phone on your side of the bed, closer to you than to your partner, and compare a night alone if you can.

Where do my recordings go?

Nowhere. Audio is stored on your iPhone only. Snorly has no accounts and no cloud upload; sharing a clip is something you do on purpose.

Is there an Android version?

Not yet. Snorly is built for iPhone. If you sleep alone and want to know whether you snore, read how to tell if you snore for a method that works with any phone.
