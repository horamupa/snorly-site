ShutEye Alternatives: 6 Sleep Apps and What They Cost

By Mikhail Manev· Published November 11, 2026· 8 min read

The best ShutEye alternative depends on which part of ShutEye you used. For snore recording without a subscription, Snorly plays every clip back for free. For sleep sounds and stories, BetterSleep has the deepest library. For a smart alarm, Sleep Cycle. For sleep tracking on an Apple Watch, Pillow or SleepWatch, because ShutEye has no Watch app. For audio plus sleep stages at a lower yearly price, Noxogy. We make Snorly, one of the six, so each row cites its App Store listing.

Key facts
  • ShutEye has 4.8 stars from about 350,000 US App Store ratings. Premium is $59.99 a year or $9.99 a month after a 7-day trial (App Store and ShutEye FAQ, 2026).
  • Noxogy lists $19.99 a year, and Pillow and SleepWatch list $39.99 (US App Store, 4 November 2026).
  • ShutEye's privacy label lists audio data; none of the six alternatives lists it (App Store, November 2026).
  • ShutEye has no Apple Watch app; five of the six alternatives list Apple Watch support (App Store, November 2026).
  • No app on this list can diagnose sleep apnea, including ShutEye's "sleep apnea risk report" (AASM 2018).

Why look for a ShutEye alternative?

People leave ShutEye for a few clear reasons, and each one points to a different app. The usual triggers are the $59.99 yearly price, the 7-day trial and its billing, audio data on the privacy label, and the lack of an Apple Watch app. If you only want one part of what it does, you can pay less.

The reviews show the split. ShutEye holds 4.8 stars on the App Store and 1.6 from 179 reviews on Trustpilot (October 2026). Both can be true: the app can work well for most people while the trial and billing frustrate some. Our full comparison is in Snorly vs ShutEye.

Best ShutEye alternative for snoring

For snoring, a dedicated recorder beats a broad sleep app. ShutEye's FAQ says "some basic features" work free but does not say whether every snore clip plays back without paying. Snorly answers that plainly: recording, playback of every clip, the score, the timeline and your history are free every night, with no night limit.

Snorly records snoring, sleep talk and coughs, counts breathing pauses and gasps per hour as something to discuss with a doctor, and keeps audio on the phone. Premium adds full-night audio, remedy comparisons, a PDF report and Apple Health import. It has no sleep sounds and no sleep stages, and only 12 US ratings so far.

If you want other snore-first apps, see SnoreLab alternatives and Snorly vs SnoreClock.

Best ShutEye alternative for sleep sounds

BetterSleep, formerly Relax Melodies, has the largest library here: more than 300 sounds and music tracks, over 250 meditations and more than 100 stories, plus its own sleep tracker and sleep recorder. It has 4.7 stars from about 390,900 ratings, the most on this page. Its label lists purchases, identifiers and usage data used to track you.

Its App Store page lists many plans between $11.99 and $99.99, so check the price on your device. If you mostly used ShutEye to fall asleep to rain sounds, this is the closest replacement, and it also runs on Apple Watch.

Best ShutEye alternative for a smart alarm

Sleep Cycle is built around its smart alarm. It wakes you in your lightest sleep phase, tracks sleep with sound or motion, and has an AI coach and a sleep sounds library. It has 4.7 stars from about 31,600 ratings. Its support page lists snore detection and sound detection as Premium features.

Its "Who's Snoring?" feature tries to separate two snorers using voice samples, which ShutEye does not document. See Snorly vs Sleep Cycle for the detail on what is free.

Best ShutEye alternative for Apple Watch

ShutEye has no Apple Watch app, so if you sleep with a Watch you have two strong options. Both track automatically from the wrist, give a sleep report and can record sound. Pillow is stronger on analysis. SleepWatch has more ratings and a coaching focus.

Pillow. 4.4 stars from about 95,800 ratings. It shows sleep stages, heart rate analysis and trends compared with Apple Health. Sound recordings, smart wake-up and heart rate analysis are Premium, at $39.99 a year or $19.99 a month. It says analysis runs on the device and works without registration. More in Snorly vs Pillow.

SleepWatch. 4.7 stars from about 328,400 ratings. It offers automatic tracking on the Watch, sleep sounds recording, snore tracking in four levels from minimal to heavy, a smart alarm and AI coaching, with a white noise library. It lists Premium at $39.99 a year or $4.99 a month, with lower offers on the same page.

Best cheaper ShutEye alternative

Noxogy is the lowest yearly price here at $19.99, with a one-time full version at $23.99. Formerly Prime Sleep Recorder, it has 4.5 stars from about 13,200 ratings. It covers sleep stages, snore detection, audio recordings, a smart alarm and lifestyle factors, and it runs on Apple Watch. Add-ons such as Sleepy Sounds cost $3.99 each.

Its listing does not spell out the free limits, so check on the first morning. If you can do without the big sounds library, that is a third of ShutEye's yearly price.

Is there a free ShutEye alternative?

Partly. Every app here is free to download, but what stays free varies. Snorly keeps recording and playback free every night. Sleep Cycle's free plan covers tracking and the smart alarm. SleepWatch says the app is free with a Premium membership for extras. For free sleep sounds, ShutEye itself says free users get more than 50 tracks.

"Free" often means a trial that renews on day 7. Set a reminder for day 6 when you start one. Cancel in iPhone Settings, under your name, then Subscriptions.

How to cancel ShutEye and switch

Cancel first, then pick your replacement, so the renewal does not catch you. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose ShutEye and cancel. Your history stays in ShutEye, since none of these apps imports it. Run the new app for three nights before you judge it, with the phone in the same spot.

What to do tonight
  1. Decide which part of ShutEye you actually use: sounds, tracking, the alarm or snoring.
  2. Pick the one app from the table that does that part best.
  3. If it starts a free trial, set a reminder for the day before it renews.
  4. Put the phone on the nightstand, plugged in, 0.5 to 1 m from your head, and review the first night in the morning.

Related questions

What is the best app like ShutEye?

Sleep Cycle is the closest all-round match, with tracking, a smart alarm and a sounds library. BetterSleep is closest if you mainly use sounds. If snoring is why you installed ShutEye, a dedicated recorder like Snorly does that part free.

Is there a free alternative to ShutEye?

For snoring, Snorly's recording and playback are free every night. For sleep tracking, Sleep Cycle and SleepWatch both have free versions with limits. No app here gives you ShutEye's full feature set for nothing.

Is ShutEye better than Sleep Cycle?

They overlap heavily. ShutEye has a larger audience and more sounds and stories; Sleep Cycle has the stronger smart alarm, an Apple Watch app and a way to separate two snorers. Both keep their best snore features behind Premium or do not document them.

Which sleep app works with Apple Watch instead of ShutEye?

Pillow and SleepWatch are built for it, and Sleep Cycle, BetterSleep and Noxogy also have Watch support. ShutEye has no Watch app and reads Watch data through Apple Health.

Can any of these apps detect sleep apnea?

No. ShutEye offers a risk report and Snorly flags pauses and gasps, but only a home sleep test or a lab study can confirm apnea. See best sleep apnea apps for what each tool measures.
