Snorly vs Sleep Cycle: Snore Recorder or Smart Alarm?

Sleep Cycle and Snorly solve different problems. Sleep Cycle is a smart alarm and sleep tracker used by millions, with snoring as one feature, and its support page lists snore detection under Premium. Snorly does one job: it saves the seconds it hears snoring and plays every clip back for free. Pick Sleep Cycle for the alarm and sleep stages. Pick Snorly if snoring is the question. We make Snorly, so check each row yourself.

Key facts
  • Sleep Cycle has 4.7 stars from about 31,600 US App Store ratings and says it has more than 40 million downloads; Snorly has 12 US ratings (App Store, 4 November 2026).
  • Sleep Cycle's support page lists snore detection, sound detection and "Who's Snoring?" as Premium features; the free plan covers the smart alarm, sleep analysis and the nightly graph.
  • Sleep Cycle's App Store page lists Premium options from $2.99 to $57.99. Snorly's Premium is $24.99 a year or a one-time lifetime purchase, and its recording and playback stay free.
  • Sleep Cycle's privacy label links name, email, coarse location and health data to you. Snorly's label lists usage data used for tracking and no audio data; it has no account, and recordings stay on the phone unless you share a clip.
  • Neither app can diagnose sleep apnea (AASM 2018).

Is Sleep Cycle free?

Sleep Cycle is free to download, and its free plan covers the smart alarm, sleep analysis and a nightly sleep graph. Its support page lists online backup, full statistics, the Sleep Aid library, snore detection, sound detection, sleep programs and "Who's Snoring?" under Premium. So the free plan tracks your sleep, but expect to pay for the snoring part.

The App Store listing agrees. It names soundscapes, sleep stories, data export, Philips Hue sunrise and Apple Health sync as Premium tools, and the subscription card reads "Unlock all sleep aids, snore detection & more". Feature lists move, so look at the Premium tab inside the app before you rely on either source.

Premium appears in the App Store at several prices, from $2.99 up to $57.99. Which one you see depends on the offer and plan. Sleep Cycle's site mentions a 7-day free trial, so set a reminder for day 6 if you try it.

Does Sleep Cycle record snoring?

Yes, with Premium. Sleep Cycle's listing says it detects snoring, coughing and sleep talking and calls itself a snore tracker and sleep talking recorder. Its support page puts snore and sound detection in Premium. You get those events in the context of your sleep graph, next to the sleep phases.

If two people share the bed, "Who's Snoring?" tries to tell your snoring from your partner's using a short voice sample from each of you. No dedicated recorder in our comparisons does that. It is also Premium, and it is the strongest reason for a couple to pick Sleep Cycle.

Where Sleep Cycle is the better choice

Pick Sleep Cycle if the alarm is the point. Waking in a light phase is what the app was built around, and Snorly has only a gentle alarm with no sleep-phase timing. Sleep Cycle also suits you if you want sleep stages, long-term sleep trends, an AI sleep coach, or an Android or Apple Watch app.

It is the better pick for couples who both snore, because of "Who's Snoring?".

Where Snorly is the better choice

Pick Snorly if snoring is the question and you want the answer without a subscription. Every clip of every night plays for free, with no night limit, and each one sits on a timeline with its loudness band. The morning review takes a minute, and you can hand a partner the proof.

Snorly also counts breathing pauses and gasps per hour, flagged as worth discussing with a doctor, and it makes a PDF report with Premium. There is no account, and recordings stay on the phone unless you share a clip. To test mouth tape or a nasal strip, tag the nights and compare them. Snorly has no sleep stages and does not run on Android. If you need those, Sleep Cycle covers more ground.

Is Sleep Cycle better than SnoreLab for snoring?

For snoring alone, SnoreLab goes deeper: a snore score, remedy tracking, a large library of remedy guides and a free tier that plays back some clips. Sleep Cycle treats snoring as one sleep metric. If you only care about snoring, a dedicated app fits better; see Snorly vs SnoreLab for how the two snore-first apps differ.

What are the best Sleep Cycle alternatives?

The right alternative depends on what you use Sleep Cycle for. For snoring, use a dedicated recorder such as Snorly or SnoreLab. For sleep sounds with tracking, ShutEye is the closest match. For sleep stages with audio at a lower yearly price, Noxogy lists $19.99 a year.

The sleep recorder comparison covers seven apps by what they keep, and Snorly vs ShutEye covers the broadest of the sleep apps.

What neither app can do

Neither app can tell you whether you have sleep apnea. A phone hears sound; apnea is measured by airflow, breathing effort and blood oxygen in a home sleep test or a lab study. The American Academy of Sleep Medicine says consumer sleep technology is no substitute for that testing. A recording of snoring, silence and a gasp is a reason to see a doctor. How to compare them in three nights

  1. Install both. With one phone, alternate nights; with two, put them side by side on the nightstand, same distance from your head, both plugged in.
  2. If you start a Sleep Cycle trial, check that snore detection is on before bed.
  3. In the morning, play the loudest snoring each app saved and check whether you could hear it without paying.
  4. Keep the app you opened at breakfast, and cancel any trial you will not use before it renews.

Related questions

Is Sleep Cycle worth paying for?

If you want a smart alarm, sleep trends, sleep sounds and snore detection in one app, many people find it worth it. If you only want to hear and track your snoring, a dedicated recorder costs less; Snorly's playback and history are free.

Does Sleep Cycle work without a watch?

Yes. Sleep Cycle tracks sleep with the phone's microphone or accelerometer and needs no wearable. You put the phone on the nightstand. It also has an Apple Watch app if you want one.

Can Sleep Cycle tell who is snoring?

Its Premium "Who's Snoring?" feature tries to. You each record a short sample, and the app uses it to separate your snoring from your partner's. Snorly has no such feature; it records all the snoring it hears from where the phone sits.

Is Sleep Cycle safe for my privacy?

Its App Store label says name, email, coarse location and health data may be linked to you and that identifiers may be used for tracking. Read the label and privacy policy if that matters to you. Snorly has no account and keeps recordings on the phone; its label lists usage data used for tracking.

Does Sleep Cycle detect sleep apnea?

No. It tracks snoring and other sounds, and its listing talks about potential sleep issues, but no phone app can diagnose sleep apnea. A home sleep test or a lab study is how apnea is confirmed.
